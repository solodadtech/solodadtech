Corkboard

Live on the web

Escanaba's local business board

A social app where verified local businesses post updates and neighbors follow along. Residents pin the businesses they care about, then like and comment on what's new around town.

  • ✓ Only businesses post — a feed that's actually useful, never noisy
  • ✓ Every business gets its own modern page — theme, hours, and updates
  • ✓ No ads from anyone else, ever
  • ✓ Local only — built for Escanaba, MI
